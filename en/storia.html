Our story

Since 1967

A peaceful, family-run corner in the heart of the morainic hills. Ever since, the same idea: simplicity, honest ingredients and a warm welcome.

Three generations

  1. 1967

    The stoves are lit

    The family opens the restaurant on the Via Mantovana.

  2. Every day

    Hand-rolled pasta

    The love-knot tortellino, as Valeggio tradition demands.

  3. Today

    The same welcome

    Groups, families, travellers: the table is always laid.

The symbol of Valeggio

The love-knot tortellino

Wafer-thin pasta folded by hand into a little knot. It is the signature dish of Valeggio sul Mincio and the heart of our cooking: with butter and sage or in broth.
