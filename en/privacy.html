Privacy notice

Privacy and cookies

How we use the data you give us when you book or write to us through the website, and which tools the website uses on your device. In short: we only use your data to handle your booking or request, we don’t sell it, we don’t do targeted advertising and the website uses no profiling or statistics cookies.

1. Who processes your data

The data controller is Mara Società a responsabilità limitata semplificata (Mara S.r.l.s.), which runs Ristorante Mara: registered office at Via Mantovana 1180, 37067 Valeggio sul Mincio (VR), Italy, VAT no. 05164120239, REA VR-475590, certified email (PEC) ristorantemara@pec.it.

For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights, write to info@ristorantemara.com or call +39 045 485 7283. No Data Protection Officer (DPO) has been appointed, because one is not required for a business like ours.

3. Allergies and intolerances

If you mention allergies, intolerances or dietary needs in the notes, this is health information. We only use it to prepare a suitable meal and only share it with the kitchen and the dining-room staff. The legal basis is your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give by ticking the relevant box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time by writing to us: in that case we delete the notes, but we will not be able to take them into account in the kitchen.

Please do not include health information in the notes that is not needed for your meal.

4. Who we entrust it to

Your data is processed by authorised and trained restaurant staff. To run the website we rely on these providers, who process it on our behalf:

  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin, Ireland): the website forms are sent to a Google service (Apps Script), bookings and requests are stored in a Google Sheet and emails are sent from Gmail. The opening hours and menu shown on the website also come from Google. Google privacy policy.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA): hosts the website pages (GitHub Pages) and logs technical browsing data for security. GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Maps, only if you choose to view the map: Google processes the data as an independent controller, under its own privacy policy.

We do not disclose your data publicly. We may only pass it to the authorities where required by law.

5. Transfers outside the European Union

Google and GitHub may also process data in the United States. Both participate in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, recognised as adequate by the European Commission, and apply the standard contractual clauses approved by the Commission.

6. How long we keep it

  • Bookings and requests, including the notes and proof of consent: up to 12 months after the date of the lunch or event, to handle any follow-up (for example a request for clarification or a new booking); they are then deleted automatically.
  • Record of cookie choices: 24 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Emails exchanged about the booking or request: for the same period.
  • Technical browsing data: for the time set by the hosting provider for security reasons.
  • Preferences saved in your browser: see the cookie table.

We may keep data for longer only where required by law or to defend our rights.

7. Your rights

At any time you can ask us to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing, receive it in a readable format (portability), object to processing based on legitimate interest and withdraw any consent given, without affecting what was done before the withdrawal (Arts. 15–22 GDPR). Just write to info@ristorantemara.com: we reply within one month.

If you believe the processing is not lawful, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live.

The website and forms are not intended for children under 14: if you are under 14, please ask a parent to book for you.

8. Cookies and similar tools

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device; similar tools include, for example, the browser’s local storage (localStorage). Our website uses no profiling, marketing or statistics cookies, and the fonts are hosted on the website itself (they are not loaded from Google Fonts).

We only use technical tools that are needed for the website to work and do not require consent, plus the Google Maps map, which loads only if you choose it.

How to choose and change your mind

On your first visit a banner asks whether you want to load the Google map. You can accept, decline or customise: if you decline or close the banner, the map does not load and the website still works. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page, or load the map just once with the “Show the map” button. After 6 months we ask you again.

You can also delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge.

The website contains links to other websites (for example Google Maps, Facebook, TripAdvisor): when you open them, their own privacy policies apply.

9. Changes

If we change the way we process data, we update this page and the date at the top; if the tools used by the website change, we ask you again for your cookie choice.
