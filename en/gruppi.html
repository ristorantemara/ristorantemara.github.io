Frequently asked questions

Is there room for the coach?

Yes: the car park is large and has room for coaches too.

Can you cater for allergies and intolerances?

Yes: mention them in the notes of your request and we’ll prepare suitable dishes for whoever needs them.

Are you open in the evening?

No, we are only open at lunchtime. Open every day from 12:00 to 14:00, closed on Tuesdays.
